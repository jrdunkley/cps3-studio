CPS3 Studio

Compose music, draw sprites, recolour characters and stages, and adjust moves for Street Fighter III 3rd Strike, then try your changes in the game.

MusicPiano roll, MIDI, mixing and instruments on the game’s own sound driver. ArtSprites, animations and colours, with PalMod and FBNeo patch sharing. MovesTimelines, attack data and collision boxes, tested against an opponent. GamePlay, pause, step and compare the game with your changes.

Bring your own ROM: Japan 990512 NO CD rev 1 (sfiii3nr1.zip with sfiii3.zip). Your files and work stay on this computer.
